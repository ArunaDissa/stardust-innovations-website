Manike · මැණිකේ · மாணிக்கம்

Built for Sri Lankan Culture.
Unlike Anything Else.

Every feature was designed around how Sri Lankans actually find life partners — through family, culture, astrology, and trust. No Western app offers this.

✦ Cultural Identity Features
🏛️

Poruwa System

Instead of swiping, send a Poruwa — a traditional proposal of interest. Inspired by Sri Lanka's sacred wedding platform. Meaningful from the very first moment.

Unique to Manike
⭐

Lagna · ලග්න Matching

Horoscope compatibility built right into the app. Match on Lagna signs the way Sri Lankan and South Indian families have done for generations — now digitised for modern life.

Unique to Manike
🌺

Kulaya · කුලය

Express your cultural heritage and community identity with sensitivity and respect. Find someone who shares your roots — handled with the nuance this topic deserves.

Unique to Manike
🔮

Western Zodiac

For those who follow western astrology, Manike includes your star sign as a separate identity layer alongside Lagna — because Sri Lankans carry both traditions.

Unique to Manike
🏅

Family Approval Badge

Toggle "මගේ පවුල දනිති" — My Family Knows — to show a deep maroon badge on your profile. A powerful trust signal for serious, family-oriented relationships.

Unique to Manike
👨‍👩‍👧

Parent Mode

Parents can browse profiles and shortlist matches on behalf of their son or daughter — the child gets final approval. Designed for how families in Sri Lanka actually work.

Unique to Manike
🔒 Safety & Trust Features
🤝

Chaperone Mode

Invite a trusted friend to silently monitor your chat before a first date. They can see the conversation and step in if anything feels wrong. Safety built into every connection.

Unique to Manike
🪪

NIC Verification

Verify your identity with your Sri Lanka National Identity Card or Passport. Verified users receive a blue badge — so you always know you're talking to a real person.

Sri Lanka Specific
🌐

Three Languages

The entire app — every screen, every prompt — is available in Sinhala, Tamil and English. Switch anytime. Your language, your comfort, your choice.

Sri Lanka Specific

Who We Are

About Stardust Innovations

Stardust Innovations is a technology company based in Sri Lanka, building mobile applications for the South Asian market and the global Sri Lankan diaspora.

We believe great technology should feel local — designed with cultural understanding, built with world-class standards, and trusted by the people who use it every day.

Every app we build is guided by three principles: safety first, privacy by design, and genuine value for our users.
