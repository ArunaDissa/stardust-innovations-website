About Stardust Innovations

Stardust Innovations is a technology company based in Sri Lanka, building mobile applications for the South Asian market and the global Sri Lankan diaspora.

We believe great technology should feel local — designed with cultural understanding, built with world-class standards, and trusted by the people who use it every day.

Every app we build is guided by three principles: safety first, privacy by design, and genuine value for our users.
