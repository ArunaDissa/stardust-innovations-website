Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Stardust Innovations Pvt Ltd ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and protects your personal information when you use our apps, including Manike.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Phone number (for authentication)
  • Name, date of birth, gender
  • Profile photos
  • National Identity Card (NIC) number and photo (for identity verification only)
  • Location (city)
  • Relationship preferences, religion, interests and other profile details
  • Messages sent through the app
  • Emergency contact details (for safety features)

Information collected automatically:

  • Device type and operating system
  • App usage data and interactions
  • Push notification tokens

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To show you compatible matches based on your preferences
  • To verify your identity using your NIC number
  • To send you push notifications about matches and messages
  • To provide safety features including emergency contact notifications
  • To process subscription payments
  • To improve our apps and services
  • To prevent fraud, abuse and ensure safety

3. NIC Verification

Your National Identity Card number and photo are collected solely for the purpose of identity verification. Your NIC number is stored securely and is never shown to other users. Only authorised Stardust Innovations staff can access NIC data for verification purposes.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only in these circumstances:

  • With other users: Your public profile (name, age, photos, preferences) is shown to potential matches. Your phone number, NIC and private details are never shared.
  • Service providers: Supabase (database), Expo (push notifications), PayHere (payments). These providers process data on our behalf under strict agreements.
  • Legal requirements: If required by Sri Lankan law or a court order.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase with Row Level Security (RLS) policies. All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. We follow industry-standard security practices to protect your information.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data at any time through the app
  • Update or correct your information in your profile settings
  • Delete your account and all associated data by contacting us
  • Withdraw consent for optional data processing

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

8. Children's Privacy

Our apps are not intended for anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we discover a minor has created an account, we will delete it immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by email. Continued use of our apps after changes means you accept the updated policy.
